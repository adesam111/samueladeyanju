Samuel Adeyanju

About

Human-environment social scientist working on conservation, rural livelihoods, and climate solutions in West Africa.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at McGill University, working remotely from Seattle. My research uses qualitative and participatory methods, including ethnography, interviews, focus groups, and case studies, to examine how rural livelihoods, common-pool resource governance, gender and equity, and conservation interventions interact in West Africa. I link community-level evidence to international conservation and climate policy.

I completed my PhD in Forestry at the University of British Columbia in 2026 as a Vanier Scholar, supervised by Professor Terry Sunderland, and my MSc at UBC as a Mastercard Foundation Scholar. Before graduate school, I earned a first-class degree in Forestry and Wood Technology from the Federal University of Technology, Akure, Nigeria. I speak English and Yoruba.
